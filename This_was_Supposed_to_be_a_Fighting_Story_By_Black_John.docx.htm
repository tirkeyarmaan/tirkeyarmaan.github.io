ARC! 1— THE EX-GIRLFRIEND INCIDENT

An absurd story about friendship, questionable decisions, supernatural trouble and people who really need to mind their own business.

CHAPTER 01 — "Two Tides and One Very Confused Man"

Location: Los Pollos Hermanos

An ordinary afternoon.

Johnny is sitting at a table, dressed in his usual dark outfit. He looks relatively relaxed.

Across from him is Divya.

She looks annoyed.

Divya: "Why are we here after everything happened...?"

Johnny: "You know I'm not having it. It's not making a little bit of sense."

Divya: "So our friendship is bothering you, bigboy?"

Johnny: "I don't care, but two tides colliding and just calming down isn't on my bucket list."

Divya: "You seriously called me here because of Kajal?"

Johnny: "I invited you here because I have questions."

Divya: "You're still as irritating as ever."

Johnny: "Consistency is important."

Divya rolls her eyes.

Johnny: "Let's get something straight. You and Kajal weren't exactly friends."

Divya: "People change."

Johnny: "People also develop common sense. Doesn't mean I expect miracles."

Divya: "Maybe we just understand each other now."

Johnny: "That's a very convenient explanation."

Divya: "Why do you even care?"

Johnny pauses.

Johnny: "Curiosity."

Divya: "You're investigating our friendship?"

Johnny: "I prefer intellectual curiosity."

Divya: "You're a creep."

Johnny: "A curious creep."

An uncomfortable subject

Divya looks at Johnny for a moment.

Divya: "You know, sometimes I wonder if you genuinely don't understand people."

Johnny: "Depends on the person."

Divya: "You really didn't notice, did you?"

Johnny: "Notice what?"

Divya: "Forget it."

She looks away.

Divya: "You never were interested in relationships anyway."

Johnny: "Still aren't particularly enthusiastic about them."

Divya: "Yeah. I remember."

There is a brief silence.

Their history is clearly more complicated than either wants to discuss.

Johnny's phone starts ringing.

INCOMING CALL — RANVEER

Johnny: "Speak."

Ranveer: "Where are you?"

Johnny: "Somewhere between heaven and bad decisions."

Ranveer: "Arman wants you to come over."

Johnny: "Why?"

Ranveer: "I don't know. He said it's important."

Johnny: "Then why are you calling me?"

Ranveer: "Because I'm important."

Johnny hangs up.

Divya: "Your friends are weird."

Johnny: "You have no idea."

Johnny stands up.

Johnny: "We'll continue this another time."

Divya: "There won't be another time."

Johnny: "That's what you said last time."

He leaves.

 

CHAPTER 02 — "The Investigation Department Has No Qualifications"

Location: Arman's meeting spot.

Arman is waiting for Johnny.

Johnny: "So what's the emergency?"

Arman: "What emergency?"

Johnny: "Ranveer called me like the government was about to collapse."

Arman: "I just asked him to call you."

Johnny: "Never outsource communication to that man again."

Arman: "Anyway, how did it go with Divya?"

Johnny: "She's still Divya."

Arman: "That explains nothing."

Johnny: "Exactly."

Arman asks about the conversation.

Arman: "Did you find out why she and Kajal became friends?"

Johnny: "Apparently, people change."

Arman: "That's it?"

Johnny: "She also called me a creep."

Arman: "Well, that's not new information."

The investigation

Johnny: "Something doesn't add up."

Arman: "Or maybe there's nothing to add up."

Johnny: "Two people who couldn't stand each other suddenly becoming best friends? That's suspicious."

Arman: "Maybe they bonded."

Johnny: "Over what?"

Arman: "I don't know. Friendship?"

Johnny: "You're not helping."

Arman: "I wasn't aware I was being paid."

Johnny looks at him.

Johnny: "You will be compensated with my appreciation."

Arman: "Keep it."

Meanwhile...

Divya and Kajal are seen together.

They seem perfectly comfortable around each other.

Johnny notices them.

Johnny: "There they are."

Arman: "They're just talking."

Johnny: "Exactly. Suspicious."

Arman: "You're making me regret coming here."

Johnny: "Friendship is a strange phenomenon."

Arman: "So is your brain."

 

CHAPTER 03 — "The Side Characters Have Entered the Chat"

Location: A familiar hangout spot.

Johnny and Arman are sitting together.

Ranveer arrives.

Ranveer: "What's up, unemployed philosophers?"

Johnny: "The circus has arrived."

Ranveer: "And the audience is ugly."

Arman: "Can we have one normal conversation?"

Ranveer: "No."

Ranveer sits down.

Ranveer: "So what's the topic today?"

Johnny: "Human relationships."

Ranveer: "Ew."

Arman: "We're trying to understand something."

Ranveer: "Then you've come to the wrong person."

Johnny: "At least you're self-aware."

Ranveer: "I am a man of many talents."

Arman: "Name one."

Ranveer: "I can sleep for twelve hours."

Johnny: "Incredible. A biological achievement."

Enter Kunal

Kunal arrives while looking at his phone.

Kunal: "Why did you guys call me?"

Ranveer: "Because we getting physical with you."

Kunal: "Motherfu-."

Johnny: "We're discussing a mystery."

Kunal: "Is it internet-related?"

Johnny: "No."

Kunal: "Then I'm already uninterested."

Arman: "It's about Divya and Kajal."

Kunal finally looks up.

Kunal: "Oh."

Ranveer: "You know something?"

Kunal: "No, but I can probably find a Reel about it."

Johnny: "That's not how investigations work."

Kunal: "That's literally how half the internet works."

Ranveer: "Thats why he never completed school."

Johnny: "Same for you."

The group

Kunal: "Maybe they bonded over mutual hatred."

Ranveer: "Enemies to besties. Peak character development."

Johnny: "That's actually concerning."

Arman: "Can we get back to the point?"

Kunal: "What point?"

Silence.

Johnny: "I've surrounded myself with intellectual disasters."

Ranveer: "And yet you keep coming back."

Johnny: "Stockholm syndrome."

 

CHAPTER 04 — "A Brief History of Bulldozers"

 

 [ Flashback — Sunday school ]

Long before the current events, there were four friends.

Johnny. Ishaan. Angel. Divya.

Johnny and Ishaan attended Sunday school together.

The four eventually formed their own group.

It wasn't some grand organisation. There were no rules, no hierarchy and certainly no official paperwork.

It was simply a group of friends.

Then other friends started joining.

Francis, Aman, Vidhi, Aakriti, Roma, Ayushi, Ashu, Daniel, Saket and Arpita.

The group grew through a simple philosophy:

Your friend is my friend too.

And eventually, the Bulldozer Gang had fourteen members.

 

The name itself had a connection to Johnny.

It became part of the group's identity, something that represented its beginnings.

The story doesn't reveal the exact naming incident yet.

[ Flashback ends, Back to Present day ]

 

Kunal: "Wait. Fourteen people?"

Johnny: "Yes."

Ranveer: "That's not a friend group. That's a small municipality."

Arman: "Fourteen people and somehow nobody can organise a meeting."

Johnny: "You should've seen the group chat."

Kunal: "I can imagine."

Ranveer: "Was there an admin?"

Johnny: "Unfortunately."

Kunal: "Who?"

Johnny: "That's classified information."

Ranveer: "Bro thinks he's the FBI."

 

CHAPTER 05 — "The Fall of the Founder"

 

[Major flashback]

Approximately one year before the Ex-Girlfriend Incident.

Scene 1 — The original conflict

Ishaan has a problem.

His two ex-girlfriends, Kajal and Divya, have confronted him.

Kajal was his girlfriend before Divya.

Divya was his more recent ex.

The situation has become increasingly unpleasant.

Ishaan contacts Johnny.

Ishaan: "Bro, I need your help."

Johnny: "What did you do?"

Ishaan: "Nothing."

Johnny: "That's never a good start."

Ishaan: "Can you just come?"

Johnny calls Arman.

Johnny: "I need you."

Arman: "For what?"

Johnny: "A social emergency."

Arman: "That sounds illegal."

Johnny: "Bring yourself."

 

Scene 2 — The confrontation

Johnny and Arman arrive.

Divya and Kajal are waiting.

Johnny: "Well, this is awkward."

Divya: "Stay out of this."

Johnny: "I'd love to. Unfortunately, my childhood friend has summoned me."

Kajal: "This doesn't concern you."

Johnny: "Then why am I standing here?"

Arman looks at Ishaan.

Arman: "What exactly happened?"

Ishaan: "It's complicated."

Johnny: "Translation: he messed up."

The confrontation escalates.

Johnny and Arman end up fighting Divya and Kajal.

The encounter ends with Johnny and Arman defeating them.

 

Scene 3 — The aftermath

The conflict has ended, but the consequences are only beginning.

Johnny's friendship with Divya is damaged.

Ishaan's position in the Bulldozer Gang is already complicated because of his history of cheating on Divya.

He becomes unofficially distant from the group.

And Johnny?

His own involvement in the confrontation puts him on a collision course with the people who once stood beside him.

 

CHAPTER 06 — "The Crown Was Never Permanent"

 

Scene 1 — The meeting

The Bulldozer Gang gathers.

The original group has changed.

Ishaan is already unofficially out.

Kajal, who once stood against Divya, has now become Divya's best friend and the newest member of the group.

The group has grown to fifteen members with Kajal's addition.

Johnny stands among them.

Divya and Angel are standing together.

Divya: "I don't think Johnny should be part of this group anymore."

Johnny looks at her.

Johnny: "Excuse me?"

Divya: "You heard me."

Johnny: "That's an interesting proposal."

Angel speaks.

Angel: "I agree with her."

Johnny turns towards her.

Johnny: "You too?"

Angel: "I think it's better this way."

Johnny pauses.

Johnny: "Better for whom?"

Neither answers immediately.

 

Scene 2 — The history

A series of flashback panels:

  • Four friends at Sunday school.
  • Johnny and Ishaan establishing the group.
  • Angel and Divya standing with them.
  • More friends joining.
  • Fourteen members becoming a familiar sight.
  • The group growing into something much bigger.

Back to the present.

Johnny looks around.

Johnny: "You know I was the one who started this. Everything and every bit of it. Even the fucking name of it! It was based on me!"

Nobody speaks.

Johnny: "You were just some delinquents here and there, and I made a generational event happen."

Absolute Silence.

Johnny: "Four people. That's how it started. Four fucking people."

He looks towards Divya and Angel.

Johnny: "And now you're going to stand here and tell me I don't belong?"

Divya: "This isn't about who started it."

Johnny: "Of course it isn't."

He looks around the room.

Johnny: "You know what? Keep it."

He adjusts his coat.

Johnny: "Enjoy your kingdom."

Johnny walks away.

Final panels

The door closes.

Angel watches him leave.

Divya remains silent.

Nobody follows him.

 

He helped build the group. He gave it a name. He watched it grow. And now he was no longer welcome.

 

CHAPTER 07 — "The Man Who Wasn't Invited"

 

Scene 1 — One year later

The main timeline continues.

Johnny is no longer part of the Bulldozer Gang.

Ishaan is also no longer actively involved.

Divya and Kajal have unexpectedly become close friends.

Kajal is now the newest member of the group.

The group continues without Johnny.

Scene 2 — Present day

Johnny is sitting with Arman.

Arman: "Do you ever miss it?"

Johnny: "Miss what?"

Arman: "The group."

Johnny: "Not particularly."

Arman: "You're lying."

Johnny: "And you're asking unnecessary questions."

Arman doesn't argue.

Johnny: "People move on. That's the whole point."

Arman: "Do they?"

Johnny doesn't answer.

Scene 3 — The paper

Arman's phone vibrates.

He checks it.

His expression changes.

Johnny: "What?"

Arman: "Nothing."

Johnny: "You have the face of someone who just discovered a tax investigation."

Arman: "We need to meet."

Johnny: "We're literally meeting."

Arman: "Not like this. Everyone."

Johnny looks at him.

Johnny: "Who?"

Arman: "Ranveer. Kunal. You. Me."

Johnny: "Why?"

Arman: "There's something important."

Johnny looks at Arman.

Johnny: "You're becoming increasingly mysterious. It's irritating."

 

CHAPTER 08 — "The Letter Nobody Asked For"

Scene 1 — The gathering

Ranveer arrives.

Ranveer: "I hope there's food. I'm not attending a meeting without food."

Kunal arrives while staring at his phone.

Kunal: "I was in the middle of something."

Ranveer: "Touch grass."

Kunal: "You literally spend your entire day arguing with people online."

Ranveer: "It's called community service."

Johnny arrives.

Johnny: "Why does this look like a disciplinary hearing?"

Ranveer: "Because you have committed several crimes against common sense."

Johnny: "And yet I'm still the most qualified person here."

Kunal: "That's debatable."

Arman is already waiting.

He doesn't join their jokes.

Johnny: "Alright. What's going on?"

Scene 2 — The paper

Arman takes out a folded piece of paper.

He places it in front of Johnny.

Ranveer: "What's that?"

Arman: "Something for Johnny."

Kunal: "A letter? Bro got mail."

Johnny: "You called an entire meeting to give me stationery?"

Johnny picks it up.

He unfolds it.

[ THE PAPER'S CONTENTS ARE PROHIBITATED. ]

Johnny reads it.

His expression changes.

Johnny: "..."

He reads it again.

Johnny: "Where did you get this?"

Arman: "That's not the important part."

Johnny: "Then what is?"

Arman remains quiet.

Johnny folds the paper and puts it away.

Ranveer: "Okay, what the hell was that?"

Johnny: "Nothing."

Kunal: "That definitely wasn't nothing."

Johnny: "You two should learn when to stop asking questions."

Scene 3 — The overheard conversation

Later, Johnny and Arman step away from the gathering.

Ranveer is nearby.

He accidentally catches part of their conversation.

Johnny: "You should've told me earlier."

Arman: "I wasn't sure how you'd react."

Johnny: "Does she know?"

Arman: "Not yet."

Johnny: "Then don't involve anyone else."

Ranveer stops.

Ranveer (thinking): "She?"

He watches them for a moment.

Ranveer (thinking): "Who the hell is she?"

He quietly walks away.

Scene 4 — The final page

Kunal notices Ranveer acting strangely.

Kunal: "What's wrong with you?"

Ranveer: "Nothing."

Kunal: "You look suspicious."

Ranveer: "I always look suspicious."

Kunal: "Fair enough."

Cut to Johnny.

He stands alone for a moment, one hand inside his coat.

The folded paper is still there.

We still don't know what's written on it.

We don't know what Arman knows.

And they haven't met the girl connected to it.
